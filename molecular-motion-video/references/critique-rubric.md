# Critique rubric (stages 6–9)

Score each item 0–2 on every contact sheet or preview. Target: no 0s, and a total ≥ 80 % before moving on.

Write fixes as numbered, testable instructions, and list what to KEEP so it is not lost.

| Area | 2 = good | Typical failure (0) |
|---|---|---|
| **Science** | Correct geometry and scale; mechanism order matches the script; colours follow convention | Wrong shape (e.g. planar sulfite); atoms jump instead of interpolating; colours swap mid-video |
| **Story** | Each scene shows exactly what the narration names, *when* it names it | Visual lags or leads the word; idle screen time |
| **Composition** | Clear subject, rule of thirds, clean negative space for the title, nothing cut by the frame edge | Subject too small (v1: final crystal at 25 % width → fixed to 40 %); clutter |
| **Light & depth** | Subject lit (key + rim), background with depth (gradient, mist, soft bokeh) | Dark "stick" molecules; flat black void; visible emitter planes with hard edges |
| **Motion** | One continuous eased camera; molecules vibrate and drift; no popping | Cuts, flashes, linear robotic moves, frozen molecules |
| **Legibility** | Captions inside safe area over a scrim; labels near anchors, no overlaps; sub/superscripts drawn correctly | Text over bright regions; missing glyphs (₂, ²⁻); labels covering the action |
| **Sound** | Natural voice, terms pronounced right; music ducked; −16 LUFS ±1; no whooshes | Robotic/flat voice; mispronounced key terms; music fighting the voice |
| **Technical** | Spec, frame count and duration right; no black or frozen glitches; qa all OK | Missing frames; wrong fps; clipped audio |

The requester's own words outrank the rubric. The reference video's feedback was "too still", "transitions bad (flicker)", "industry part looks like architecture-software preview", "liked the material part", "robotic voice" and "transition sounds bad". Each became a non-negotiable above.
